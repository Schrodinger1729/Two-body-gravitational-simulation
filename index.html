THE PHYSICS, SIMPLY

Gravity does the math.

Each body pulls on the other. No prescribed paths, just a pair of masses and the rules of the universe.

01 / FORCE
F = G m₁ m₂/r²

Newton’s law: the gravitational force grows with mass and weakens with the square of the distance.

02 / ACCELERATION
a₁ = G m₂r̂/ r²

Divide force by each body’s own mass. Both accelerate toward their shared center of mass.

03 / MOTION
x, v → x′, v′

Velocity Verlet advances position and velocity in small steps for stable, energy-aware orbits.
